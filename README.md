# Customer_churn_Analysis
📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis
🧠 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn behavior for a telecom company to identify key factors driving customer attrition and provide data-driven retention strategies. The goal is to reduce churn, improve customer retention, and increase customer lifetime value.

🎯 Problem Statement

The telecom company is facing a high customer churn rate of 26.54%, leading to significant revenue loss and increased customer acquisition costs. This project aims to analyze customer behavior, identify churn drivers, and recommend actionable strategies to improve customer retention.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Python (Pandas, NumPy)
Data Visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn)
Jupyter Notebook
Business Intelligence Thinking
(Optional extension: SQL, Power BI, Machine Learning)
📂 Dataset Information
Total Customers: ~7000+
Target Variable: Churn (Yes/No)
Features include:
Contract type
Internet service
Payment method
Tenure
Monthly charges
Additional services (Tech Support, Security, etc.)





📌 Key Insights
🔴 1. Overall Churn Rate
Churn Rate: 26.54%
~1 in every 4 customers is leaving the company
🔴 2. Contract Type (Strongest Driver)
Month-to-month churn: 42.71%
One-year contract churn: 11.27%
Two-year contract churn: 2.83%

👉 Customers with shorter contracts are significantly more likely to churn.

🔴 3. Internet Service Impact
Fiber optic churn: 41.89%
DSL churn: 18.96%
No internet: 7.40%

👉 Fiber optic users represent the highest-risk segment.

🔴 4. Payment Method Impact
Electronic check churn: 45.29%
Credit card auto-pay: 15.24%
Bank transfer auto-pay: 16.71%
Mailed check: 19.11%

👉 Manual payment users show significantly higher churn.

🔴 5. Tenure Analysis
Churned customers average tenure: 18 months
Retained customers average tenure: 38 months
Majority churn occurs within the first 10 months

👉 Early customer lifecycle is the most critical retention period.

🧠 Business Interpretation
Customer churn is primarily an early-stage lifecycle problem
Long-term contracts significantly improve retention
Automated payment methods improve customer loyalty
Fiber optic customers may face unmet service expectations
Weak onboarding contributes to early churn
🧩 Root Cause Analysis
🔴 1. Low Contract Commitment

Customers on month-to-month plans have low switching barriers.

🔴 2. Payment Friction

Electronic check users show weak engagement and higher churn.

🔴 3. Service Quality Gap

Fiber optic users may experience pricing or service dissatisfaction.

🔴 4. Poor Early Engagement

Customers tend to churn within the first year due to weak onboarding.

🔴 5. Lack of Value-Added Services

Absence of services like Tech Support and Security increases churn risk.

💡 Business Recommendations
🟢 1. Increase Long-Term Contracts
Promote one-year and two-year plans
Offer discounts and loyalty benefits
🟢 2. Improve Early Customer Experience
Strong onboarding program
First 6–12 month engagement strategy
Welcome support initiatives
🟢 3. Fix Fiber Optic Churn
Improve service quality
Reduce customer complaints
Offer bundled discounts
🟢 4. Promote Auto-Pay Adoption
Encourage credit card and bank transfer payments
Offer cashback or rewards
🟢 5. Bundle Value-Added Services
Tech Support
Online Security
Device Protection
🟢 6. Target High-Risk Customers

High-risk profile:

Month-to-month contract
Fiber optic users
Electronic check payment
Tenure < 12 months
📊 Final Business Insight

Customer churn is primarily driven by contract type, payment method, internet service type, and early customer lifecycle behavior. The highest-risk customers are new users on month-to-month contracts using electronic payments and fiber optic services.
